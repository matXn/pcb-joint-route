----------------------------------------------------------------------
DECLARATION OF OPEN HARDWARE INTENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The ESPiezometer project is released as open hardware to enable free replication, modification, and scientific validation. All design source files are provided in editable formats without DRM or proprietary restrictions.

Users are encouraged to fork, adapt, and improve the design, provided that:
- Original attribution is preserved.
- Hardware license terms (CERN-OHL-P/W) are respected.
- Firmware and software derivatives remain under GPLv3.
- Documentation credits are maintained when redistributed.
